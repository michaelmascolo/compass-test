## Purpose-adaptive teaching — same concept, different function

Confirmed by inspecting invitations: the SAME writing concepts are taught, but their FUNCTION shifts with purpose, with no template imposed.
- **Explanatory (case 5):** thesis/opening taught as *what the reader should understand by the end* — explanation function, not a claim to defend.
- **Analytical (case 13):** teaches interpretation — *what mood a specific image creates* — not a persuasive claim.
- **Narrative (case 17):** teaches narrative meaning — *the one moment you want the reader to feel* — not a thesis.
- **Reflective (case 21):** honors uncertainty as the heart of reflection and moves toward insight; NOT forced into a fixed claim.
- **Mixed (case 28):** explicitly names the essay's DUAL job (explain + argue) and helps coordinate the two purposes rather than collapsing to one.
