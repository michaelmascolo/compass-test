## Functional evaluation across paragraph conditions

- **Effective paragraphs (1–5):** honored and named for the function they already perform (argumentative distinction, analytical interpretation, narrative atmosphere, reflective insight, explanatory mechanism); invited forward, not restructured.
- **Weakly organized (6–10):** purpose diagnosed as *unclear/not yet cohering* and the student invited to name the paragraph's job — coherence treated as meaningful organization, not transition-smoothing.
- **Competing purposes (11–14):** the engine surfaced the multiple jobs crowded into one paragraph and asked which should govern — no template imposed.
- **Incoherent (15–17):** identified that sentences do not hold together toward one purpose; invited a controlling idea.
- **Weak development (18–21):** purpose identified but restatement (not development) named; student invited to develop via the resource the purpose calls for — without the AI supplying content.
- **Typed moderate paragraphs (22–29):** narrative/explanatory/analytical/argumentative/reflective/comparison/bridge/concession — development interpreted relative to purpose; bridge and concession paragraphs read for their relationship to the whole.
