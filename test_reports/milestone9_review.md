## Transitions as relationships, not vocabulary

- **Strong flow, few transition words (1–4):** the engine identified the relationship the writer achieved (cause-effect, elaboration, contrast) and honored it — it did NOT tell the student to add transition words. (Cases 1 & 21 required the strong-flow applies fix below.)
- **Many transition words, weak coherence (5–8):** the engine saw through the connective vocabulary and named the relationship as unclear/undetermined — coherence judged by whether ideas actually relate, not by the presence of 'firstly/however/therefore'.
- **Weak sentence-to-sentence (9–12) & weak paragraph-to-paragraph (13–16):** correct level identified; the student invited to make the intended relationship legible, without the AI supplying connective content or rewriting.
- **Purpose-typed (17–29):** relationships read appropriately per purpose (persuasive concession-then-contrast, explanatory causal/chronological sequence, analytical technique→effect, narrative chronology-to-realization, reflective temporal-to-meaning, mixed cause-effect→judgment).
